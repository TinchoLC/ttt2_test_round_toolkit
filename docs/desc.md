[b]Test Round Toolkit[/b] is a standalone testing addon for TTT2.

It is inspired by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3306937352][b][TTT/2] Test Round Initiator[/b][/url], but it does [b]not reuse any of its code[/b] and does [b]not require the original addon[/b] to work.

Open [b]F1 → Test Round Toolkit[/b] to quickly test roles, weapons and other addons without constantly restarting or reconfiguring the server.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] Native TTT2 F1 integration
[*] Solo test rounds
[*] Change your own role and health
[*] Support for custom TTT2 roles
[*] Spawn weapons from TTT2's equipment database
[*] Automatic respawning
[*] Extra shop credits
[*] Extended test rounds
[*] Damage information
[*] Admin-only session controls
[*] Admin-only bot management
[/list]

[h2]Commands[/h2]

[code]
!testround
!<role>
!health <amount>
!bot <amount>
!unbot
!clean
[/code]

All administrative actions, including starting or stopping test sessions, cleanup and bot management, are validated server-side.
